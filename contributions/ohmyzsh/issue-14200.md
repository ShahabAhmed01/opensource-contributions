## Semantics

The `.deb` arm previously `mkdir -p`'d `control`/`data`, then recursed
into `extract ../control.tar.*` / `extract ../data.tar.*` from inside
them. The inner `extract` derives the extraction dir from the member's
basename, so a package with ≥2 control members came out double-nested
(`pkg/control/control/{conffiles,control,postinst}`), while the data
side avoided the nesting only when `data.tar.*` had exactly one
top-level entry (the single-content lift moved it up, `pkg/data` dropped
out) — the layout varied with member count.

The patch keeps the `ar vx` member unpack and extracts each member
tarball directly into the prepared result directory (`tar … -C
control`/`-C data`), reusing the plugin's own per-compression chains
(`pigz`/`pbzip2` `-I` invocation, `tar --xz --help` probe +
`xzcat|tar`/`zstdcat|tar` fallbacks). dpkg shippable member formats
(gz/xz/zst, legacy bz2) all covered; unexpected member formats are
reported like the RAR/7z arms do. `success`/`$?`, `-r` remove and
`--to-directory` flows keep their previous semantics (pre-existing
failure-masking by the trailing member cleanup is unchanged upstream
behaviour and is called out honestly in the PR scope note).
