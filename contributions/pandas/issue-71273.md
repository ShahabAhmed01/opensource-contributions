## Issue

https://github.com/pandas-dev/pandas/issues/71273
"BUG: to_datetime and Timestamp give opposite offsets for 'UTC+0300'"

Filed 2026-10-17 by `digit50`, OPEN, labeled `Needs Triage`, no
assignee, zero comments at selection (2026-10-24 ~06:56Z). The report
was complete with per-spelling output, root cause pointers
(`guess_datetime_format` token rewriting at parsing.pyx lines
1016-1040 and the final `strftime(guessed_format) == rebuilt` check),
and a suggested fix.

Behavior: for `"2020-01-02 08:07:13 UTC+0300"` (and `UTC+03:00`,
`GMT+03:00`, `UTC-0300`), `pd.to_datetime(s)` used a guessed `%z`
format and applied the nominal sign (+03:00 / −03:00), while
`pd.Timestamp(s)`, `format="mixed"`, and the no-guess spelling
`UTC+3` all went through dateutil's POSIX interpretation of a
zone-name-prefixed offset ("UTC+0300" means −03:00) — the same text
yielded two instants a full offset apart.

## Root cause

dateutil interprets a numeric offset directly after a zone name with
the POSIX (inverted) sign; `strptime` applies the nominal sign to
`%z`. `guess_datetime_format` normalizes the offset token to
`strftime("%z")` output and validates by comparing
`strftime(guessed_format)` against the *rebuilt* string, so the
guessed format was self-consistent with dateutil's parse of the
rewritten tokens yet WRONG for `strptime` applied to the original
string: `to_datetime` then shifted the instant by double the offset.

## Fix

- `pandas/_libs/tslibs/parsing.pyx`: in `guess_datetime_format`, after
  the parse and before token rewriting, detect `<zone letters>
  [ \t]*<sign><1-2digit hour>[mm][ss]` at the string end
  (`_timelex_zone_offset`, module-level regex), compute the nominal
  offset, and return `None` when dateutil's actual
  `parsed_datetime.utcoffset()` disagrees with it (POSIX-inverted or
  space-separated offset dropped). No format can represent these
  strings, so the guesser declines and every consumer
  (`to_datetime` without format, `Timestamp`, `format="mixed"`)
  parses via the same dateutil fallback. Zero offsets
  (`"UTC+0000"`, GH#66827 context) keep their guesses.
- Tests:
  - `pandas/tests/tslibs/test_parsing.py::test_guess_datetime_format_zone_with_offset`
    — 5 params (UTC+0300, UTC+03:00, GMT+03:00, EST+0330, "UTC
    +0300") assert the guess declines;
    `test_guess_datetime_format_offset_without_name` and
    `test_guess_datetime_format_zone_with_zero_offset` guard the
    unchanged behaviors ("+0300" without a name still guesses
    `… %z`; `UTC+0000` still guesses).
  - `pandas/tests/tools/test_to_datetime.py::TestToDatetimeInferFormat::test_to_datetime_zone_with_offset`
    — 6 params asserting `to_datetime` == `Timestamp` ==
    `format="mixed"` scalar and Series paths for every spelling,
    including the previously already-correct `UTC+3` and
    `UTC +0300`.
- whatsnew entry under Timezones in `doc/source/whatsnew/v3.2.0.rst`
  referencing `(:issue:`71273`)`, lexically sorted per the repo's
  `sort-whatsnew-items` hook.
